Research

First author · Manuscript, 2026

Integrative Single-Cell Transcriptomics and Explainable AI for Disease Mechanism and Biomarker Discovery in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer

Tanjidul Huda, Sriporna Biswas

Re-analysis of ClonMapper-barcoded MDA-MB-231 cells (GEO GSE329526) separated into invasive and non-invasive fractions. Sample-level differential expression, XGBoost with whole-sample hold-out, a QC-only baseline, held-out SHAP and GSEA. The invasive state was reproducible across independent transwell samples and involved EMT-related (TGFBR2, VIM, HMGA1), inflammatory (CCL2, CXCL2) and KRAS/E2F programmes.

cells 15,294 held-out AUC 0.92 QC-only AUC 0.63 DE genes (FDR<0.05) 6,651
Code: doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23494607 GitHub repository
Second author · Preprint, bioRxiv 2026

An Interpretable Machine Learning Framework for Early- versus Late-Stage Classification of Breast Cancer from Gene Expression, with Co-expression Network Analysis

Sriporna Biswas, Tanjidul Huda

TCGA-BRCA RNA-seq (UCSC Xena), 65-gene a priori panel, four classifiers with leakage-safe preprocessing, permutation importance, FDR-corrected differential expression and a co-expression network. Reports a limited but measurable stage signal, stated with confidence intervals.

tumours 1,075 test ROC-AUC 0.618 95% CI 0.530–0.704
